CalorIA Scan 2.0.7: your protein, your meals and your privacy, clearer than ever

A new Protein tracker, faster scans, editing for meals you've already logged, swipe to delete and clearer privacy —everything new in the 2.0.7 update.

By CalorIA Scan · October 19, 2026 · 2 min read

The short version

  • New Protein tracker: tap your protein on Home and see whether it comes from chicken, beef, pork, fish, eggs, dairy or plant-based sources.
  • You can now rename a meal, edit the portion of one you've already logged, and swipe to delete meals.
  • Privacy is clearer: express consent, optional analytics and a more complete account deletion.

New: Protein tracker

Tap your protein on Home and see where it comes from: chicken, beef, pork, fish, eggs, dairy or plant-based sources. Today's view is free; with Pro you also see your sources over the last 7 and 30 days.

Your AI nutritionist (Pro): a short daily tip on the variety of your protein sources, in the personality you picked: Profesional, Chilango or Abuelita.

Faster scans

Your results now appear up to 2 seconds sooner.

Name it your way

Tap the pencil next to your meal's name to fix it before saving. Type a different protein (say "pork" instead of chicken) and the ingredients and calories update automatically. Plus 20 new proteins to swap in: thigh, wings, skirt steak, pork chop, cochinita, salmon, tilapia, tuna, beans, lentils and more.

Edit a meal you already logged

Only ate half? Open a meal from today and adjust the portion (½ to 5) with the same selector you use when scanning: calories, macros and grams recalculate instantly. You can rename it too. Tap "Save changes" and it also updates in your cloud backup.

Swipe to delete meals

On Home, swipe a meal left and confirm to remove it. It's also removed from your cloud backup, and it no longer comes back when you reopen the app.

Clearer privacy

  • Express consent: we clearly ask permission to use your nutrition data, and separately (optional) for your weight, height, age, sex and activity level. Withdraw it anytime in Profile → More → Withdraw consent.
  • Optional analytics: in the EU, UK and Switzerland it stays off until you turn it on. Anywhere, you can turn it off in Profile → More.
  • More complete account deletion: we also disconnect "Sign in with Apple" and erase your purchase data with our payments provider.
  • Updated privacy policy on October 17, 2026: caloriascan.com/en/privacy-policy.html

And also

  • A quick tour shows you the key features the first time you use the app.
  • The bottom menu now follows the language you pick.
  • The "Already have an account? Sign in" button is bigger and easier to find.
  • You can now leave the camera screen even when the camera can't start.

How to update

Version 2.0.7 will be available on the App Store starting October 20, 2026. When you see it, update CalorIA Scan and tell us what you think. Every comment helps us improve.
